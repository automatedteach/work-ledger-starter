Classification rule: heavy at `>=40,000` measured `o200k_base` tokens or upon a documented consequential mandatory-context failure. The post-split 36,000-token-derived byte warning and file/open-loop warnings remain routine signals; no byte or word proxy may decide cap compliance. If exact tokenization cannot run, that determination and adoption are blocked, although a documented consequential failure still selects heavy mode. Adoption readiness requires a fully conserved result below `40,000`; a result at or above the limit remains provisional and blocked.

Heavy mode requires a separate Deep TRACER trace and the complete heavy review below. Routine mode may mark heavy-only fields `Not applicable — no heavy trigger`.
