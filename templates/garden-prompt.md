# Run a Work Ledger Garden

Follow `AGENTS.md` and `PROTOCOL.md`, especially Protocol sections 7, 26, and the TRACER gateway. This template is operational guidance, not independent authority.

1. Complete trusted initialization and create the exact garden dossier before protected inspection.
2. Start the textual TRACER trace. Classify the run as routine or heavy before proposing changes.
3. Measure the exact Protocol section-7 joined startup corpus with the pinned published `o200k_base` tokenizer. Heavy mode applies at 40,000 measured tokens or after a documented consequential mandatory-context failure. The post-split byte warning derived at 36,000 tokens is a routine prompt for exact measurement, never the limit. There is no word-count fallback; if exact tokenization cannot run when required, leave cap compliance and adoption blocked. A documented consequential failure still selects heavy mode.
4. Exact measurements are required after the protocol split and again at release, when the byte warning is recalibrated. If heavy mode applies, use Deep TRACER and `templates/heavy-garden-review.md` in a dedicated dossier. The adoption-ready candidate must measure below the exact 40,000-token limit. If semantic conservation prevents that, retain meaning and report a blocked over-limit state; the limit is never deletion authority.
5. Inspect narrowly in order: materially stale workstreams; principles/decisions; possibly unreported events; contradictions/stale claims; compression/routing. Declaration stays permissive. Staleness fires only when a declaring dossier has exact `Dossier status: complete YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.fffZ` later than the row's exact UTC-millisecond `Last reviewed` timestamp and records nonempty material impact on status, next action, routes, or claims that the row does not reflect. Mere declaration, consultation, or mechanical work does not fire; combine several stale rows into one question when useful.
6. From that inspection, retrieve a bounded, exact TRACER batch by projected claim, exact ID, dependency branch, reverse dependent, or material relation. Make the first garden question, numbered question 1, the owner-facing claim-and-relationship card: exact coverage and retrieval limits; each latest claim ID/version, proposition, stance, confidence, and contention; load-bearing dependencies and reverse dependents; conflicts, identity/equivalence, supersession/reassessment, and other material relations; `none recorded` categories; and suspected gaps or misrepresentation. With no relevant registered claims, say so, distinguish legacy or unregistered prose, and ask whether that absence is expected. Confirmation alone does not churn a version; an owner correction is authority about meaning, not automatically factual evidence.
7. Ask no more than eight questions total per batch, including the TRACER card. Each question states the exact uncertainty, consequence, evidence routes, proposed default, and `No answer = no semantic change`.
8. Map every accepted answer to exact paths and operations before editing. Keep one writer. Route any actual claim or relationship change through the protected staged-tree procedure and substantive review, binding acceptance to the exact `git write-tree` OID. Any staged-tree or declared-surface mutation invalidates review, and the committed tree must equal the accepted staged tree.
9. For heavy mode, freeze the pre-edit baseline and run all three independent conservation lenses: baseline coverage, logic/identity, and provenance/retrieval. Rerun affected work after every revision and unconditionally audit the final whole candidate.
10. Preserve active authority, qualifications, unresolved conflicts, identity, and durable routes. A plausible alternative restores the affected unit and becomes a numbered A-B owner choice stating the exact difference, consequence, affected paths, and evidence routes.
11. Validate and remeasure, but never treat validation or size reduction as semantic proof. Owner adoption and Git authority remain separate.

`Run the garden` never authorizes external retrieval or action. An unattended
schedule may send only this reminder, then stop without repository inspection,
dossier creation, source access, or mutation:

```text
The weekly Work Ledger garden is ready. Reply `Run the garden` when you want me to inspect the current ledger and prepare a short, proposal-first agenda. I have not read or changed the repository or accessed an external source in this scheduled wakeup.
```

Scheduling is user/surface configuration, not repository-shipped authority or
evidence of a project-attached task. A human calendar reminder is equivalent.
